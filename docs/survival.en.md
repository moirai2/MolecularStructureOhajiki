# MolSurvival (Element Survival)

[日本語](./survival.md) | **English**

> 🎉 **Chaotic party survival.** Simultaneous flicks let luck and skill ruin everyone equally — somebody always loses a marble. A fast, ridiculous, laugh-out-loud game.

Everyone starts with 10 marbles. Every time your flicked marble doesn't make it back, you lose one. Hit 0 and you're out. Last player standing **wins**.

- **Players:** 3–4 recommended (2–5 also OK; with 5 players all 5 marble colors are used)
- **Time:** 10–20 min
- **Age:** 6+ (for younger kids, use [Variant 1 "Carbon Count Mode"](#variant-1-carbon-count-mode-for-kids))

> 📘 **Marble colors and how to read a card → [COMPONENTS.en.md](./COMPONENTS.en.md)**
> 📗 **Chemistry terms → [GLOSSARY.en.md](./GLOSSARY.en.md)**

## Setup

1. **Marbles:** Each player picks 1 of the 5 colors (black / white / red / blue / yellow) and takes 10 of that color. No two players may share a color

2. **Center grid:** Shuffle the Lv1 deck and lay **15 cards face-up in a 5×3 grid** (the remaining 5 cards go into the Lv1 reserve deck). This is the "board"

3. **Element cards:** Place 10 element cards along the **top and bottom edges of the grid** (5 each — C / H / O / N / Other, any order). **Orient each set of 5 so the labels are readable from the player on that side.** These cards form each player's **launch area** (outside the board, not on it)

4. **Reserve decks:** Stack the remaining cards beside the board (left or right) by level
   - **Lv1 reserve:** 5 cards
   - **Lv2 reserve:** all 20
   - **Lv3 reserve:** all 10

5. **Seating:** Players sit freely on the top or bottom edge of the board (any combination of neighbors / opposite seats). Each player places marbles on the **near side of their element cards (the side where the kanji label points toward them)** and flicks from there (the card surface is slippery, which helps). **Launcher cards are NOT used in standard play** (they're a handicap-only tool — [details](#distance-handicap))

   - **2 players:** 1 on top, 1 on bottom (face to face)
   - **3 players:** 2 top / 1 bottom, or 1 top / 2 bottom
   - **4 players:** 2 on each side (standard, shown in the diagram)
   - **5 players:** Split 3 / 2 across the sides (tight quarters — arrange seats so elbows don't clash). **5-player games are crowded, causing more collisions and off-board exits — leaning the game further toward party-chaos mode**

See the diagram for a 4-player setup ([larger view](./survival_setup.svg)).

## Round flow

### Phase ①: Flick

All players flick **simultaneously**.

- Someone calls "**3, 2, 1, GO!**"
- On "GO!", everyone flicks 1 marble from the **near side of their element cards** (the side with the kanji label facing them)
- **Landing check:** If the marble is resting on any molecule card on the **board (the 5×3 center grid)**, it is **on the board**. Element cards, the mat, and the table are all **off the board** (element cards are physical cards but outside the board — landing on them still counts as off-board). Check by looking from the side.
- **Marble straddling multiple molecule cards (quantum mechanics model):** When a marble physically touches multiple cards, it counts as **landed on all of them** (skilled players can intentionally straddle to dodge ② collision or ③ max-element). The card it "really" landed on is not decided in advance — the player picks the card at the moment of the survival check (observation collapses the state). **For younger players, the simpler ruling "whichever card it overlaps most" cuts down arguments**

### Phase ②: Survival check

Resolve the 3 checks below **in order ① → ② → ③**. **As soon as one triggers, the round ends.** Later phases are skipped. Players caught by the trigger lose a marble; **everyone else returns their flicked marble to their hand**.

| Order | Phase | Trigger | Result |
|-------|-------|---------|--------|
| ① | **Off-board** | At least 1 player landed off the board (on an element card, the mat, or the table) | All off-board players lose 1 marble. **Everyone else returns their marble to hand.** Resolution ends |
| ② | **Collision** | No one triggered ①, and 2+ players landed on the same molecule card | All players on the collided card lose 1 marble. **Everyone else returns their marble to hand.** Resolution ends |
| ③ | **Max element** | Neither ① nor ② triggered (everyone is on a different molecule card) | **Among the molecule cards that were landed on**, the player on the card with the **highest element total** loses 1 marble (all tied players lose if there's a tie) |

> ⚠️ **Only one phase fires per round.**
> If anyone is off-board, ② and ③ are skipped. If no one is off-board but there's a collision, ③ is skipped. **So you can flick boldly without fear of stacking penalties.**

> **What is "element total"?**
> The sum of atom counts in a molecule.
> Examples: H₂O total = 3 (2 H + 1 O); CH₄ total = 5; C₂H₂ total = 4; C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose) total = 24.
> More on this in [GLOSSARY.en.md](./GLOSSARY.en.md).

> **Worked examples (4 players)**
> - **Example 1 (① off-board fires):** A is off-board; B & C land on H₂O; D lands on CH₄
>   - ① A is off-board → **only A loses 1.** B/C/D get their marbles back (② and ③ skipped)
> - **Example 2 (② collision fires):** No one off-board; A & B both land on H₂O; C on C₆H₆; D on CH₄
>   - ① no one off-board; ② A & B collide on H₂O → **A & B lose 1 each.** C/D get marbles back (③ skipped)
> - **Example 3 (③ max element only):** Everyone on a different card. Max total is C₆H₆ (total=12), held by A
>   - ① and ② don't fire; ③ A holds the max → **only A loses 1**
> - **Example 4 (③ all-tied):** All 4 players on different cards with the same total
>   - ① and ② don't fire; ③ all tied for max → **everyone loses 1**

Players who reach **0 marbles are eliminated**, but can continue with [the "Heckler mode"](#heckler-mode-for-eliminated-players) if they want.

### Phase ③: Removal & refill

> 🔥 **Important rule: every molecule card that was landed on this round is discarded, regardless of who survived.**
> Cards that produced a safe landing, cards involved in collisions, cards holding the max — all go to the **discard pile** at end of round (never back to a deck). Element cards (the launch area) are not consumed. This means the safe low-total cards get cleared out, and Lv2/Lv3 cards refill in their place — the board gets progressively more dangerous. **This is the heart of the game.**

Refill empty slots: **while the Lv1 reserve has cards, refill from Lv1**. Once Lv1 is empty, refill from Lv2; then Lv3 (decks are consumed as queues, not per-slot).

## Ending the game

End the game by either condition:

| Condition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Only 1 player remains | That player wins |
| All reserve decks are exhausted | The player with the most marbles in hand wins (tie = shared) |

## Heckler mode (for eliminated players)

Players who hit 0 marbles and are out of the main game can keep flicking if they want.

- Borrow 1–2 marbles of **your original color** from the supply (everyone already knows you're out, so no need to change colors)
- Flick on the same call ("3, 2, 1, GO!") as the surviving players
- **Heckler flicks are entirely ignored by the survival check:** even if you land on a card, you're not part of any collision or max-element check; off-board returns your marble (hecklers can never be penalized)
- If a heckler's marble ends up on the board, remove and return it after the check
- If a heckler physically knocks a surviving player's marble, the surviving player **laughs and forgives it as part of the heckling** (no re-flicks)

> A "rescue mode" that's more fun than watching. Mess with the board, just practice your flicks, jump in and out as you like.

## Strategy tips

- Target cards with low element totals (H₂, N₂, total=2) to avoid ③ max-element
- High-total cards (Lv3 especially) are tempting to rivals → high risk of ② collision OR ③ max → a **danger zone**
- **Off-board guarantees you lose 1.** Element cards count as off-board too — over-flick and you pay immediately. On the flip side, if someone goes off-board, **everyone else is safe** (off-board becomes "the sacrifice for the others")
- Late game, when Lv2/Lv3 cards have filled in, element totals skyrocket. Everyone fights over the few low-total cards left — collide on them and ② fires; avoid them and ③ fires. There's no easy out

## Variants

Pick the variants together before play.

### Variant 1: Carbon count mode (for kids)

For young players who find "element total" tricky, replace the ③ check with a **carbon-only** count.

- ① Off-board and ② collision are unchanged
- ③ Max element → **among the landed-on cards, the player on the card with the most carbon (C) loses** (all tied lose if tied)
- **If every player has the same carbon count (including all C=0) → all players tie for the max and all lose 1** (no "everyone dodges chaos" loophole — this is the heart of the chaos)

→ All you need to count is "the number of green/black (carbon) marbles." **Age 6+.**

### Variant 2: Turn-based mode (strategy-focused)

Players flick one at a time, clockwise from the start player. Later players see earlier landings, giving them an edge.

- You may deliberately hit other players' marbles **to move them around on the board** (offensive strategy)
- **If you push another player's marble off the board:** **YOU** (the attacker) lose 1 marble. The victim and everyone else are unharmed. The round ends there (no normal ① ② ③ cascade)
  - **Pushing multiple players off at once still only costs you 1 marble** (attacker penalty doesn't stack)
  - **If your own marble also goes off-board at the same time, still only 1 marble lost** (not double-counted)
  - → If you're going to attack, **move them within the board**. Knocking them fully off costs you
  - → Skilled play: push an opponent's marble from a low-total card onto a high-total card, sticking them with ③ max risk
- **If nobody pushed anyone off-board:** After all turns are taken, resolve ① off-board → ② collision → ③ max element as the normal cascade
- **If your own flick fails (you went off-board on your own):** Normal ① off-board; you lose 1 at the end-of-round resolution
- **Start player rotation:** Later flicks are advantageous, so the start player rotates **counter-clockwise** by 1 each round

→ Balance between aggression and precision. Pushing opponents fully off-board hurts you, so careful control matters. **Age 10+.**

### Variant 3: Blindfold mode (party-style)

Block your sight at the moment of flicking — chaos for laughs.

- On your flick, **close your eyes** (or cover them with your palm)
- Flick as usual from the near side of your element cards (the landing is mostly luck)
- Survival check runs normally
- Apply to everyone / only to some players (as a handicap) — both work

→ Closes skill gaps dramatically, so it's also a good handicap for mixed-age groups or strong flickers.

### Variant 4: Team mode (4 players only)

2v2 team play.

- Split the 4 players into 2 teams
- Each player uses an individual color (4 colors total)
- Survival check is still resolved per-player
- When a team has all members eliminated, the other team wins
- Within a team, talking strategy is allowed

→ Great for families or classroom collaboration. **Age 10+.**

## Handicap rules

For matchups with skill gaps. Agree on handicaps before starting.

### Distance handicap
- Only the stronger player adds a **launcher card outside** their element cards and flicks from there (longer distance = harder)

### Off-hand handicap (most practical)
- The stronger player flicks with their **non-dominant hand**

### Resource handicap
- The stronger player starts with **7–8 marbles** (fewer lives)

### Blindfold handicap
- Only the stronger player uses Variant 3 (blindfold mode)

### Suggested combinations

| Matchup | Recommended handicap |
|---------|---------------------|
| Adult vs. age 6–8 | Off-hand + distance |
| Adult vs. age 9–12 | Off-hand only |
| Veteran vs. beginner (same age) | Distance or resource |
