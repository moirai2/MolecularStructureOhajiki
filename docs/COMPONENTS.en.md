**Total:** 50 marbles (3–4 player games need 2 sets)

**Size:** 15–18mm

> **Why are "black" and "white" actually "green" and "light blue"?**
> Commercial ohajiki marbles aren't sold in black or white, so we substitute green and light blue. All rule documents still use the nominal terms **"black (carbon)"** and **"white (hydrogen)"**.

> **What is "Other"?**
> Elements other than carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, and nitrogen (sodium, chlorine, sulfur, fluorine, etc.) are grouped into a single "Other" category to simplify the game.
> For more on chemistry terms see [GLOSSARY.en.md](./GLOSSARY.en.md).

### 3. Molecule cards

Sorted by molecular complexity into 3 levels.

**Total:** Lv1 × 20, Lv2 × 20, Lv3 × 10 (50 cards in total)

#### How to read a card

Every card uses the same layout. The diagram below uses the actual Water card as an example.

![Card layout](./card_layout.svg)

##### Information used in the game (required)

| # | Position | Content | Example |
|---|----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Top edge | **Element counts** (5-color bar) | Left → right: Carbon, Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Oxygen, Other |
| 2 | Upper center | **Molecule name** (Japanese / English) | 水 / Water |
| 3 | Center | **Structural formula or illustration** | H–O–H, etc. |
| 4 | Lower center | **Molecular formula** | H₂O |
| 5 | Top right | **Functional group points (fg pt)** | Integer 0–6 (hidden if 0) |

##### Flavor data (educational only, not used in play)

| # | Position | Content | Example |
|---|----------|---------|---------|
| A | Top left | **Category icon** | Natural / Amino acid / Functional group / Synthetic, etc. |
| B | Middle right | **CAS ID / molecular weight** | 7732-18-5 / 18.0 g/mol |
| C | Bottom edge | **Short description** | "Essential solvent for life…" or similar 1–2 line note |

**How to read molecular formulas:**
- `H₂O` = 2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen (Water)
- `CO₂` = 1 carbon + 2 oxygen (Carbon dioxide)
- `C₆H₁₂O₆` = 6 carbon + 12 hydrogen + 6 oxygen (Glucose)

Subscripts are the atom count. No subscript means 1.
See [GLOSSARY.en.md](./GLOSSARY.en.md) for a fuller chemistry primer.

#### About functional group points (fg pt)

A score that represents how "strong" or "complex" a molecule is.
In **MolOhajiki (main game)**, exceeding a total of 10 fg pt triggers the end of the round.

- Lv1: mostly 0 pt
- Lv2: 1–4 pt
- Lv3: 2–6 pt

## Nice-to-have items

**Drawstring bag:**
- Needed for blind-drawing ohajiki marbles
- Available at 100-yen shops (Daiso, Seria, etc.) and on-site at Game Market
- Any color or pattern is fine

**Non-slip mat:**
- Laminated cards slide easily on a table — a mat underneath makes play much smoother
- We recommend **Daiso's non-slip mat** (110 yen), easy to size to your table
  - Product page: https://jp.daisonet.com/products/4940921836738
