# Chemistry Glossary (for players)

[日本語](./GLOSSARY.md) | **English**

This page explains the chemistry terms that appear on MolOhajiki cards, written for players who haven't studied chemistry.
You don't need to read this to play — but knowing it makes the game's world more fun.

---

## Elements

**The smallest building blocks of matter that can't be broken down further.**
Everything around us is built from combinations of these tiny pieces.

The game uses 5 element categories.

| Category | Symbol | Marble color | Everyday example |
|----------|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Carbon | C | Black (actually green) | Pencil lead, diamond |
| Hydrogen | H | White (actually light blue) | Water, traces in the air |
| Oxygen | O | Red | Part of the air you breathe |
| Nitrogen | N | Blue | About 80% of the air |
| Other | Na, Cl, S, F, etc. | Yellow | Salt, fluoride, sulfur, etc. |

Note: "Other" actually contains many different elements, but they're grouped into a single yellow marble to keep the game simple.

---

## Molecules

**Units of "stuff" made by combining elements.**
Water, sugar, air — all of them are made of molecules.

Examples:
- Water (H₂O) = 2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen
- Carbon dioxide (CO₂) = 1 carbon + 2 oxygen

---

## How to read molecular formulas

Symbols like "H₂O" or "C₆H₁₂O₆" on the cards are called **molecular formulas**.

### Reading rules

- **Letters** = element symbols (H = hydrogen, C = carbon…)
- **Small subscript numbers** = how many of that element are in the molecule

### Examples

| Formula | Composition | Name |
|---------|-------------|------|
| H₂O | 2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen | Water |
| CO₂ | 1 carbon + 2 oxygen | Carbon dioxide |
| CH₄ | 1 carbon + 4 hydrogen | Methane |
| C₆H₁₂O₆ | 6 carbon + 12 hydrogen + 6 oxygen | Glucose |

No subscript means **1** (e.g., the O in H₂O is 1 oxygen atom).

---

## Functional groups

**Parts of a molecule that give it specific properties.**
Things like "-OH" (hydroxyl) or "-COOH" (carboxyl) are written with a **hyphen (-)** before the formula.

The hyphen means "another molecule attaches here."

Examples:
- If 1 of the H atoms in methane (CH₄) is replaced with -OH → it becomes methanol (CH₃OH)
- The game includes several functional group cards like methyl (-CH₃) and hydroxyl (-OH)

---

## Functional group points (fg pt)

The number on the top right of a card. It's an **in-game score showing how "strong" the card is**.
Molecules with more complex or unusual functional groups score higher.

- Lv1 cards: mostly 0 pt
- Lv2 cards: 1–4 pt
- Lv3 cards: 2–6 pt

In **MolOhajiki (main game)**, exceeding a total of 10 fg pt triggers the end of the round.

---

## Levels (Lv1 / Lv2 / Lv3)

Cards are split into 3 tiers by complexity.

| Level | Content | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Lv1** | Simple molecules and functional groups (up to ~3 atoms) | H₂O, CO₂, -OH |
| **Lv2** | Mid-sized molecules (~5–10 atoms) | CH₄, ethanol, benzene |
| **Lv3** | Complex molecules (10+ atoms) | Glucose, caffeine, TNT |

Higher levels need more marbles to acquire, and tend to score higher.
