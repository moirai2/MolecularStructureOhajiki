# Molecular Structure Ohajiki

[日本語](README.md) | **English**

A board game that combines *ohajiki* — a traditional Japanese flicking game using small glass marbles — with the molecular structures of chemistry. Learn while you play.

**Exhibited at Game Market 2026 Spring (Tokyo)**

---

## At a glance

- **Players:** 1–6 (depends on the game; [MolOha](docs/molohajiki.en.md) is 2-player only, [MolSaba](docs/survival.en.md) is best with 3–4, [MolSugo](docs/molsugo.md) is 2–6)
- **Age:** 10+ ([TicTacMol](docs/tictacmol.en.md) and [MolSaba](docs/survival.en.md) can be played from age 6; [MolPuzzle](docs/puzzle.en.md) is recommended for age 12+)
- **Play time:** 10–60 minutes (depends on the game)
- **Genre:** Dexterity × Engine-building × Science-themed educational game

---

## Getting started

1. Check **[Component list](docs/COMPONENTS.en.md)** first to learn how to read the cards and which colors the ohajiki marbles are
2. Read the rules for the game you want to play (links in [Game list](#the-seven-games))
3. If a chemistry term is unclear, see **[Chemistry glossary](docs/GLOSSARY.en.md)** (you can play without reading it)

Start with **[TicTacMol](docs/tictacmol.en.md)** (2 players, 15 min, simple rules) or **[MolPuzzle](docs/puzzle.en.md)** (1 player, puzzle format) for your first game.

## Components (one set)

Full details and the molecule-card checklist → **[Component list](docs/COMPONENTS.en.md)**

- **Molecule cards:** 50 (Lv1 × 20, Lv2 × 20, Lv3 × 10)
- **Element cards:** 10 (5 types × 2)
- **Player cards:** 2 (used as flicking launchers)
- **Title card:** 1 (front: title + credits, back: QR code)
- **Card total:** 63
- **Ohajiki marbles:** 50 (10 each of 5 colors: green, light blue, red, blue, yellow)
